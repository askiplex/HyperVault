Executive Summary

HyperVault unifies Android storage, security, mobility and wearable trust

HyperVault is an intelligent Android platform from ASKIPLEX Technologies that brings content-centric storage, duplicate virtualization, contextual security, wearable-aware authentication, context-aware mobility and emergency response into one continuous trust ecosystem.

Why it matters

A missing intelligence layer for modern mobile users

Android users manage growing volumes of photos, videos, documents, app data and sensitive media across disconnected tools. HyperVault creates a unified intelligence layer so users can discover, protect, transfer and trust their content without breaking normal workflows.
